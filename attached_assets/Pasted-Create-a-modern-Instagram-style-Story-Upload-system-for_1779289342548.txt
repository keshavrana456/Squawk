Create a modern Instagram-style Story Upload system for the Squawk app with smooth UI, realtime updates, and premium mobile experience.

STORY UPLOAD UI:

* Add a horizontal stories section at the top of the home feed similar to Instagram.
* First story card should be:

  * user profile picture
  * “Your Story”
  * plus (+) icon
* Clicking it should instantly open story upload options.

UPLOAD OPTIONS:
When user clicks “Your Story”:

* open bottom sheet/modal with:

  * Camera
  * Gallery
  * Text Story
* Mobile-first fullscreen experience.

DIRECT CAMERA FEATURE:

* Open device camera directly inside app.
* Support:

  * photo capture
  * video recording
  * front/back camera switch
  * flash toggle
  * hold to record video
  * tap to capture photo
* Add smooth camera animations similar to Instagram.

GALLERY UPLOAD:

* Allow selecting:

  * images
  * videos
* Support multiple aspect ratios.
* Auto optimize and compress media before upload.

STORY EDITOR:
After selecting/capturing media, open fullscreen story editor.

Add:

* drag & resize text
* emojis
* GIFs
* stickers
* drawing tool
* music support placeholder
* mention users (@username)
* hashtags
* location tags
* story filters
* brightness/contrast controls
* crop/zoom support

MENTION FEATURE:

* Users can mention other users in stories.
* Mentioned users should:

  * receive notification
  * see “Add to Your Story”
  * re-share story to their own story
* Keep original creator attribution visible.

UPLOAD EXPERIENCE:

* Add upload progress bar.
* Add smooth loading animation.
* Show “Story Uploaded Successfully” toast.
* Instantly update story feed after upload without refresh.

STORY VIEWER:
Create Instagram-like fullscreen story viewer:

* tap left/right to navigate
* hold to pause
* progress bars at top
* swipe down to close
* smooth transitions
* autoplay stories

Add:

* story replies
* emoji quick reactions
* story viewers list for owner
* seen status
* mute stories option

BACKEND:

* Upload media to Cloudinary
* Store story metadata in Supabase
* Save:

  * userId
  * mediaUrl
  * caption
  * mentions
  * viewers
  * createdAt
  * expiresAt
* Stories should auto expire after 24 hours.

PERFORMANCE:

* preload next story
* lazy load media
* compress uploads
* prevent UI lag
* optimize mobile responsiveness

UI STYLE:

* Instagram-inspired polished design
* smooth modern animations
* dark mode support
* rounded modern UI
* premium mobile experience
* maintain Squawk branding and theme

GOAL:
Make Squawk stories feel as smooth, modern, and interactive as Instagram stories while keeping the app’s unique identity.
